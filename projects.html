AppFolio
An 80-unit portfolio owner came in with a 3-year gap in trust account reconciliation that two prior bookkeepers had missed. Delivered a detailed SOP to prevent recurrence.

02 → Isolated misclassified owner contributions — rebuilt ledger from that point forward
03 → Month-by-month reconciliation forward to current period — all verified clean
